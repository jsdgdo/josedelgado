Phone Number Purchase Flow

Brand: Toky
Role: Product Designer
Timeline: 2 Months

Overview

Buying a virtual phone number involves navigating complex regulatory requirements across different countries. This project focused on simplifying that complexity into a transparent, user-friendly purchase flow that guides users through document submission and verification.

The Challenge

Users were dropping off during the "Regulatory Requirements" step because they didn't know which documents were needed until after they'd started the purchase. There was also a lack of clarity around verification timelines, leading to high support ticket volume.

The Solution

We redesigned the flow to show requirements upfront based on country selection. We also introduced a real-time verification tracker and improved the internal admin tool for faster document processing. This resulted in a 40% increase in successful number activations.
