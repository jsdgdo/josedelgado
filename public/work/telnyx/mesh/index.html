Mesh Mobile

Role: Product Designer
When: April 2022

The Opportunity

Telnyx’s Wireless offering is expanding, so we were looking into expanding its network infrastructure with it. Come Mesh Mobile: a home-first distributed network solution that rewards Gateway holders (that would provide service for Telnyx SIM Cards) with a coin developed in-house.

The goal

The experience was set as follows:

  • Offer the hardware (Gateways) in an exclusive website, under a new brand
  • Have an in-gateway webapp to set up the local crypto wallet and link the user’s wallet
  • A Mesh Mobile Portal, on which you’ll be able to see all your gateways and collect your rewards

This case will focus on the Mesh Mobile Portal. This is the most important part of the experience, because it ties the whole thing together.

Research

Because we wanted to give a face to the concept, and quickly, the most research I could work on was competitive analysis. Although there was a couple of companies doing the same thing. But I honestly wasn’t aware of what I thought was the most interesting part of the project: The crypto reward.

There were gaps in functionality for most people doing the same, they were strong technically but the user (if it wasn’t savvy in web3) would get lost easily. We wanted to be the most transparent possible but also make what we were doing accessible.

I also came across some great insight into designing for web3 environments:

  • Decision fatigue is amplified when it comes to web3 products. There are so many new applications being born constantly, in a race to capitalize within the system, but the users can’t keep up.
  • Users take too many new roles. They no longer just consume or produce the data, they have to take ownership and care of it, in various ways.
  • Speed of development is exponential. Given how open building on the blockchain is, the speed of development is exponential, so bad actors will outnumber the good ones.
  • Build for a world on which multiple systems can exist together interdependently and thrive.
